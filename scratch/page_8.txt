●​ Step 3.4: Line-by-Line Comparison: The agent compares the unit prices line-by-line across all 
returned quotes. It produces a comparison matrix showing each item, the quoted prices from each 
supplier, and the percentage variance between them. 
●​ Step 3.5: Rebate & Net Cost Calculation: The agent factors in any known annual or quarterly rebates 
for each supplier (stored in the system and updated periodically). It calculates the true net cost per 
supplier after rebates are applied, which may change the ranking. 
●​ Step 3.6: Cable Price Volatility Check: For cable items specifically (which are known to fluctuate 
significantly), the agent compares the quoted prices against the last three months of cable pricing 
data from previous orders. If prices have moved significantly, it flags this to the estimator with a 
recommendation on whether to lock in now or wait. 
●​ Step 3.7: Best Package Recommendation: The agent identifies the most cost-effective supplier for 
the overall package and presents a summary showing the total cost from each supplier (pre- and 
post-rebate), along with a clear recommendation. 
●​ Step 3.8: Human-in-the-Loop Negotiation Flag: The agent highlights specific line items where a 
non-selected supplier is significantly cheaper (e.g., light switches at £1 from Supplier B vs. £3 from 
the recommended Supplier A). It generates a negotiation brief for the commercial team, suggesting 
they approach the preferred supplier to match on those specific items before confirming the order. 
●​ Step 3.9: Specialist Item Routing: If certain items (e.g., specialist light fittings, solar PV mounting kits, 
specific manufacturer products) are not stocked by the main wholesalers or are significantly cheaper 
direct from the manufacturer, the agent flags these for separate procurement and excludes them 
from the main package comparison. It can also route specialist items to sub-contractors for pricing 
where required. 
●​ Step 3.10: Final Population & Handover: Upon final supplier selection by the commercial team, the 
agent populates the chosen unit prices into the master tender spreadsheet. It also generates the 
second fix takeoff sheets per plot type that will be sent to the chosen supplier for order fulfilment 
once the project is won. The agent advises finance/commercial of the agreed costs against the 
takeoffs. 
Module 4: Tender Document Generation 
●​ Trigger: The master tender spreadsheet is fully populated and the estimator marks the tender as 
'Ready for Submission'. 
●​ Step 4.1: Data Extraction: The agent extracts the final pricing, quantities, plot types, material 
specifications, supplier details, and labour allowances from the completed spreadsheet, including the 
Accommodation Schedule data (plot numbers, house types, beds, tenure, specification, garage 
types).
